Our Story

Founded in 2018, Habesha Eatery brings the rich culinary heritage of Ethiopia to Bole. Every dish is prepared with time-honored recipes, locally sourced spices, and a passion for hospitality. From Doro Wat to fresh injera, we serve comfort and community.
